## 1. What Cam Trew actually does (copy the mechanics, not the persona)

| Layer | Cam’s playbook |
|-------|----------------|
| **Cadence** | **4–5 posts/week** · ~2h/week claimed |
| **4-3-2-1** | **4 posts/wk:** 2 education · 1 story · 1 combo · **3 pillars:** TAM reach (AI/productivity) · growth stories · subtle sales · **2 audiences:** buyer + engager · **1 lead magnet/wk** |
| **Hooks** | Contrarian line + **image = part of hook** · deliver after click · no bait-and-switch |
| **Topics** | Broad enough to spread · swipe viral formats · X/news trends |
| **Build in public** | Quit job → SaaS ($62k/mo narrative) · document journey |
| **Monetization** | **Kleo** (AI LinkedIn writer) — most posts funnel to tool |
| **Engagement** | End with question · daily comments |

**What he optimizes:** **Impressions** and **Kleo signups**.

**Gap:** Generic “AI + LinkedIn growth” — easy to blend in. **Proof-light** for technical buyers (Sapient, FDE, film clients). **Fortune-telling** adjacency (reach = $52k ads).

## 3. Your 4-3-2-1 (adapted)

### 4 posts per week (~90 min + 15 min/day comments)

| Slot | Type | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| **Mon** | **Teach** | One concrete how-to (agent, Codility pattern, platform ad diff) |
| **Wed** | **Story** | Failure/win: hackathon, Sapient path, Glows, Nebius slice |
| **Fri** | **Build in public** | 30–60s **video** or screenshot: Dracula, 11s spec, askagent |
| **Sun** | **Combo** | Contrarian take + **one CTA** (portfolio / Topmate ebook / DM open) |

### 3 pillars (content — not Cam’s generic TAM)

| Pillar | Reach hook examples | **Proof you attach** |
|--------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Ship** | Agents, Play apps, FDE-style integrations | GitHub · demo GIF · “what broke” |
| **Short-form / AI film** | Saree/challenging categories · multilingual · not AI-slop | **11s link** · 3-frame carousel |
| **Career / leverage** | Pre-hire project lever · days not months · IP safeguard | Mini case · no ROAS claims |

### 2 personas

| Persona | Who | What they need from you |
|---------|-----|-------------------------|
| **Buyer** | DTC founder, jewellery/Amazon seller, studio PM | Trust strip · demo · Book |
| **Employer** | Sapient, FDE, product eng hiring manager | Shipped repos · honest scope · video of you thinking |

### 1 lead magnet per week (rotate)

- **Platform ad matrix** (Amazon ≠ Etsy ≠ Insta) — 1-page PDF from [value props doc](./11s-site-value-props-no-roas.md)

## 4. Hooks that beat generic Cam templates

**Cam pattern:** bold claim · ad-cost math · “sign up for Kleo”

**Your patterns (pick one per post):**

1. **Show first:** “This 11s hook took 2 days, not 2 months.” + **thumbnail**  
2. **Contrarian honest:** “I don’t promise ROAS. I promise upload-ready 9:16.”  
3. **Lever:** “Jewellery brands still book shoot days for catalog Reels. We built the other path.”  
4. **Engineer:** “FDE interviews aren’t LeetCode — they’re ‘deploy in their VPC.’ Here’s how I’d scope it.”  
5. **Process:** “Human QC on every export — or I won’t put your brand on it.”

**Rules:** Line 1 = stop scroll · **Image/video = hook** · Last line = **one** question or **one** link · No fake metrics.

## 5. Weekly workflow (better than “scroll X + Kleo”)

| When | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| **Sun** | Plan 4 topics from pillars; pick 1 lead magnet | 20 min |
| **Mon** | Draft Mon + Wed posts; **record Fri clip** same session | 40 min |
| **Tue–Sat** | **15 min/day** — 5 thoughtful comments on **buyer/employer** posts | 15×6 |
| **Before post** | Hook test: would **Sapient TA** or **jewellery founder** care? | 2 min |

**Volume (Diandra):** One **long** piece → 4 posts:  
- Hackathon learnings → teach + story + build clip + combo  
- Glows lesson → teach + story + …

**Conversion:** Every **2nd** post soft-links **11s** or **GitHub**; every **4th** post explicit **“open to FDE / eng roles”** during job push (**CC-5**).
