## Rules

| Use on site | Avoid (fortune telling / perf marketing) |
|-------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Deliverables** (MP4, captions, 9:16) | Guaranteed **ROAS**, CPA, % returns |
| **Timeline** (days vs months) | “**Skyrocket** growth,” “winning ads” as outcome |
| **Process** (revisions, QC, disclosure) | “This **will** go viral / rank on Amazon” |
| **Ownership** (IP, files) | BusinessMunim-style **358% ROAS** unless **your** verified case |
| **Fit** (language, platform, category) | “**Perform like** 10× budget” as **promise** (OK as **look** metaphor with portfolio proof) |
| **Clarity** (which ad for Etsy vs Amazon) | CORRECT LEADS™, CMO, **we run your spend** |

**Psychology:** People **buy** when they believe (1) **this is for me**, (2) **I won’t get burned** (ghosting, stolen IP, embarrassing AI), (3) **I get a finished thing soon** — not when you predict their revenue.

## Scoring rubric (out of 10)

| Column | Question |
|--------|----------|
| **Memory stick** | After one scroll, can they **repeat one phrase** to a co-founder? |
| **Buy pull** | Does it **reduce fear** or **increase confidence** enough to click Book / DM? |
| **Not fortune telling** | Is the claim **about your work**, not their future metrics? (10 = safe) |
| **Buy score** | `(Memory + Buy pull + Not fortune telling) / 3` — rank by this |

## What sticks in the audience mind (one sentence each)

These are the **phrases buyers remember** — not metrics.

1. **“They won’t sell my saree look to another brand.”** → IP safeguard + challenging categories  
2. **“Hindi ads without cheesy English AI.”** → Multilingual  
3. **“Won’t look like obvious AI on my shop.”** → Natural + QC  
4. **“Done in days, and they’ll actually revise.”** → Days + revisions  
5. **“Amazon video isn’t the same as my Reel.”** → Platform recommendations  
6. **“Build a character world, not one throwaway clip.”** → Own IP  

**Does not stick (and you don’t offer):** “358% ROAS,” “your CMO,” “guaranteed winners.”

## Final stack — what makes them **buy** (site order)

### Above the fold (memory + buy)

1. **Portfolio video** (proof first — Mithun/BM both lean visual)  
2. **One line:** *Customer · Emotion · Product* **or** *Vertical video that doesn’t look like template AI*  
3. **Trust strip (5):** Days · Revisions · **IP safeguard** · Natural · Cost (soft)

### Book CTAs (test clicks — highest buy score)

| Priority | Section | Why they pay |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| **P0** | `brand-avatar` + `multilingual` | **Specific pain** — hardest to get elsewhere |
| **P1** | `platform-ads` | **Decision relief** — not predicting sales |
| **P2** | `own-ip` + `hooks-and-film` | **Aspiration** — needs strong Dracula/spec proof |

### Footer / FAQ

- Creative only — **you** run ads and metrics  
- If they want leads/ROAS → pair with media/perf (e.g. BusinessMunim lane), not you

## Fortune telling vs honest swap (copy)

| Don’t say | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| “Winning ads” / “scale ROAS” | “**Upload-ready** ads for **your** account” |
| “We’ll skyrocket growth” | “**First cut in days**; **revisions included**” |
| “Guaranteed Amazon rank” | “**Listing-style vs Reel-style** — we recommend from category research” |
| “10× performance” | “**Cinematic look** without **six-week** studio wait” (show reel) |
| “358% returns” | **No case study** until **you** have permission + data |
