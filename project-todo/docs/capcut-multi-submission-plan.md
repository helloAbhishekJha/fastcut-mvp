# CapCut World LA — multi-submission plan

**Challenge:** [#1000WaysToCCW](https://contra.com/community/topic/capcutworldlachallenge) · **Deadline:** Mon **Oct 27, 2026 ~12:29 PM IST**  
**Rules recap:** Each Contra post = **15–60s** video + **CapCut project link** + **workflow breakdown** + **social URL** (`#CapCutWorld` `#CapCutWorldLA`, @ tags). Multiple **unique** entries allowed ([Contra FAQ](https://help.contra.com/en/articles/11647374-challenges-on-contra-a-new-way-to-earn)).

**Shared production rules (entries 2–6)**

| Rule | How we enforce it |
|------|-------------------|
| **On-brief arrival** | Every SKU ends on **CapCut World LA** (stage, stadium gate, portal exit, crowd) — not a generic music video with no CCW beat |
| **No monitor portal** | **Banned trope for all SKUs:** entering CCW through a **computer/phone screen**, “CapCut app on a monitor,” or keyboard-journey **into a display**. Use physical/magical portals, doors, sport tunnel, runway scrim, manga panel tear — not **#1000WaysThroughMyMonitor** |
| **Scene continuity** | No jump cuts that feel like unrelated clips: **match cuts**, shared **color grade LUT**, **character lock sheet**, **last frame of shot N → reference for shot N+1** |
| **Multi-prompt craft** | One prompt ≠ one entry: **scene bible** (10–15 lines) + **per-shot prompt blocks** (camera, lens, lighting, wardrobe, action, continuity note) logged for Contra workflow |
| **CapCut-native** | Final polish + **WELCOME TO CCW** skill (where it fits) + documented in-app gens |
| **Unique entries** | Different concept, edit, and social post per submission |

**Master prompt log template (copy per entry)**

```text
Scene bible: [era/style, palette, aspect 9:16, fps]
Character lock: [face, hair, outfit tokens — repeat verbatim each shot]
Shot 01 (0:00–0:04): ...
Shot 02 (0:04–0:08): CONTINUITY: end pose of 01 = ...
...
Arrival beat (final 8–12s): CCW [stage/stadium/portal] + on-screen #1000WaysToCCW
```

## Submission 2 — Japanese manga / anime · music-driven story

**One-line:** Anime protagonist’s **performance or chase** crashes through a **manga panel / ink ripple** and **lands on the CCW stage** mid-song.

| Beat | Time (target 45–55s) | Visual |
|------|----------------------|--------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:04 | Manga SFX frame **ドン** — character close-up, bold ink outlines |
| 2 | 0:04–0:14 | **Music verse** — city rooftop or train, cel-shade + speed lines |
| 3 | 0:14–0:22 | Chorus hit — **panel borders animate**, world “tears” |
| 4 | 0:22–0:35 | **Arrival:** ink → neon **CCW** crowd; character still singing |
| 5 | 0:35–0:50 | Tag: JP + EN title card, hashtags |

**Style locks:** High-contrast anime, limited palette (2 accent colors), consistent **eye/hair** tokens in every prompt.  
**Music:** CapCut library or one licensed track; cut on **downbeats**.  
**Complexity:** Min **6–8** chained shots (not 1 prompt).  
**Arrival twist:** “Wildest way” = **break out of manga into live CCW**, not travel to LA.

## Submission 3 — Sci-fi · **CapCut helper robots** (daily life montage → stadium)

**One-line:** Cute-faced **CapCut-branded robots** (soft chassis, **CC** logo chest) in **professional uniforms** per job — home, office, factory, driver — then **team arrives** at a **CCW mega-stadium** for “opening ceremony.”

| Segment | Robot role | Wardrobe / set |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| A | **Home bot** | Apron, smart home, morning routine |
| B | **Office bot** | Blazer, desk, slides on hologram |
| C | **Manufacturing bot** | Hard hat + cleanroom coat, assembly line |
| D | **Driver bot** | Cap, EV interior, calm commute |
| E | **Arrival** | All four **merge** via conveyor/portal into **stadium tunnel** → CCW floor |

**Style locks:** Same **face module** (big eyes, small mouth) across robots; only **uniform** changes. Pixar-adjacent 3D or polished 2.5D — pick one and never mix.  
**Continuity:** **Match-cut** on robot **eye blink** between segments; shared **time-of-day** progression (dawn → day → golden hour → stadium lights).  
**Prompt plan:** **4 job scenes × 2 shots each** + **2 arrival shots** = 10 prompts minimum.  
**Arrival twist:** They don’t fly — they **ride a CapCut “render pipeline”** into the venue.

## Submission 4 — Heritage · **Ramayan-era** design (respectful, stylized)

**One-line:** **Stylized ancient India** (not photoreal deities): a **storyteller / warrior archer silhouette** travels via **divine chariot of light / forest path** and **steps through a lotus portal** onto **CCW stage** as “another yuga’s gathering.”

| Guardrail | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Tone** | Epic, painterly, **museum / mural** aesthetic — avoid mockery or anachronistic memes |
| **Design** | Terracotta + indigo + gold; **temple geometry** in backgrounds; **no** modern logos until final CCW beat |
| **Music** | Traditional-inspired bed (licensed) or CapCut ambient epic |

**Beats (50–60s):** Forest dawn → chariot/light path → **bridge / river wide** → lotus portal opens → **CCW** as “great assembly” (crowd as silhouettes, not religious tableau).  
**Complexity:** **8+** shots; wardrobe and **bow/silhouette** locked; continuity via **same sky gradient** and **dust motes** direction.  
**Arrival twist:** **Wildest arrival** = **yuga-spanning portal**, not airplane.

*If unsure on symbolism, keep figures **anonymous epic silhouettes** + narrator card (safer for judging and audience.*

## Submission 6 — **Fashion brand** · runway complexity (multi-look, one thread)

**One-line:** Fictional haute label **“CCW Atelier”** — **one model**, **4–5 looks**, each **impossible in one prompt** (fabric physics, jewelry, set change), continuous **runway walk** through **sets that morph** until **final look debuts on CCW stage**.

| Look | Complexity hook | Continuity trick |
|------|-----------------|------------------|
| 1 | Structural shoulders + wind | Model **same face/hair**, turn at mark |
| 2 | Liquid-metal dress | **Match on model eye line** |
| 3 | Cultural fusion piece | Walk **through light column** |
| 4 | Techwear + LED | **Behind pillar wipe** |
| 5 | Finale cape | **Reveal CCW crowd** behind scrim |

**Style locks:** **Editorial lighting** (key + rim), **35mm** language, **no** face drift — use **reference frame** each gen.  
**Prompt plan:** **Per look: wide + detail + walk transition** (15+ prompts documented).  
**Arrival twist:** Final runway **extends into CCW** — fashion week **is** the arrival.
