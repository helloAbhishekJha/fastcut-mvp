# Pre-hire project lever — “more valuable than 10 people”

**For:** CC-5 job apply · CC-7 resume · CC-63 FDE · portfolio demos before interviews  
**Principle (Abhishek, 28 Oct 2026 IST):** Before targeting a company, build (or ship a **credible slice**) of something that **automates or replaces a slow human-heavy workflow** they still run — so one hire + your tool ≈ **outcome of many roles**, with **positive business impact** you can explain without ROAS fortune-telling.

---

## The pattern

| Era / domain | Old way (many people) | Your lever | Why it wins interviews |
|--------------|-------------------------|------------|-------------------------|
| Web (historical) | Hand-coded sites, agency team | **Website builder** → automation | One builder replaced **many** implementers |
| Jewellery DTC (example) | Shoot on models, studio, editor, social poster | **AI try-on / model pics + scheduled social pipeline** | Cuts **shoot + post** headcount cadence; faster catalog → feed |
| Generic | Repetitive ops across roles | **Tool, agent, or pipeline** on *their* stack | Shows **FDE + product** mind: diagnose workflow → ship |

**Not:** vanity demos, generic todo apps, or “I used ChatGPT.”  
**Yes:** named workflow, named users inside the company, **before/after** (time, steps, or roles affected).

---

## Decision filter (pick projects)

Score each idea **1–5**; build only if **total ≥ 18** and **Business ≥ 4**.

| # | Question | 1 (weak) | 5 (strong) |
|---|----------|----------|------------|
| 1 | **Workflow** — Does it replace a **multi-person** chain (shoot → edit → post → list)? | Single nice feature | Full chain shortened |
| 2 | **Target company** — Can you name **who** at *that* employer still does this manually? | Generic “any SaaS” | Specific division/product (e.g. Sapient client vertical, jewellery brand) |
| 3 | **Business** — Can you argue **revenue/cost/speed** without fake metrics? | Vague “efficiency” | Clear: days→hours, fewer shoots/quarter, more SKUs live |
| 4 | **Demo** — **2-min video** or live path a hiring manager gets? | Slides only | Clickable + one real output |
| 5 | **You** — Uses your stack (agents, video, integrations) credibly? | Copied tutorial | Your repos + QC story |

---

## Jewellery example (template pitch)

**Problem:** Daily social + marketplace needs **model shots**; physical shoots don’t scale with SKU count.

**Lever:** Ingest product still → **AI model/drape** (challenging category) → **human QC** → export **9:16 + listing ratios** → optional **schedule** to Insta/Amazon formats (platform-specific — see [11s-site-value-props-no-roas.md](./11s-site-value-props-no-roas.md)).

**Value story (honest):** “One operator with this pipeline can publish **more angles per week** than a small shoot crew for **catalog-style** work — not replacing brand hero campaigns.”

**Interview line:** “I didn’t apply with a resume only; I built the automation your client’s merchandising team is still buying agencies for.”

## Resume / LinkedIn one-liner

> Build **workflow automation** that replaces **multi-role manual chains** (creative, ops, or delivery) — show **before/after** and a **live demo**, not metric promises.

---

## Do not

- Claim you **eliminated 10 jobs** — say **“capacity of ~N roles”** or **“work that used to need shoot day + editor + poster.”**
- Fake ROAS or guaranteed sales (same rule as 11s site).
- Ship only during the interview — **link in application** + 90s Loom.

---

## Checklist before applying to a named company

- [ ] Identified **one manual chain** at that company (or their typical client)  
- [ ] Built **minimum lovable pipeline** (even spec data)  
- [ ] Wrote **3-sentence business case** (time, cost, scale — no fake %)  
- [ ] Added link to **CC-7** resume + application CSV (Mac `job-search/`)  
- [ ] Optional: tie to **CC-25** portfolio section if creative vertical
