# Weekly rhythm — job, learning, purchases, Sunday block

**When:** Applies from 2026-10-25.

## Daily (phone-first)

- **Job applications** — outbound, CSV updates, recruiter replies (not batched to Sunday only).
- **Learning** — courses, MOOC proof, DSA (default: phone or short blocks).
- **Purchases** — study spend, tools, subscriptions (decide on phone when needed; no “Sunday-only shopping” pile-up).

## Nights (resume after day job / errands)

- Deeper **job** follow-ups, portfolio edits, LinkedIn if not done on phone.
- **Learning** labs or problems that need laptop.

## Sunday (“stable Sunday”)

Reserved for **home logistics** and **one creative ship**, not for cramming all apps/learning:

| Block | What |
|-------|------|
| Morning / midday | Washing, ironing, life admin that needs home |
| Afternoon | **One digital asset** OR **one hackathon slice** — **not both stacked in 2–4 PM IST** until Abhishek confirms Sunday calendar |
| Evening onward | Back to **job + learning** rhythm (same as week nights) |

## Cloud agents

- Do **not** assume “jobs = weekends only”; cloud build queue stays separate.
- Human **applications.csv** and **DSA** are **daily phone + night**, with Sunday add-ons above.
